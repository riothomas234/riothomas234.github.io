Experience

AIxBio Research Fellow

July — September 2026

ERA

  • I wrote a workshop paper outlining the threat model and cryptographic design for a tamper-proof log in benchtop DNA synthesisers. Using a cryptographic merkle tree and trusted platform module, we can provide certain security guarantees against malicious operators who synthesize a viral DNA strand and want to modify the records to hide it.
  • This fellowship empowered me as a researcher. Personally, I am better at picking up knowledge on the path to completing a goal, rather than because it's on the syllabus, e.g. It's much easier to me to be interested in thermodynamics after I've read the frontier papers on designing new batteries, which require thermodynamics. I learnt how to scope out & read field literature in this fellowship.
  • The people here were great to be around. Because of this, I consider work culture to be a much bigger factor in where to work. I think a big part of work are the bonds formed between people aiming for a common goal.

Summer Work Placement

July — August 2023

Moog Inc.

  • These guys made slip rings that connect circuits across rotary junctions, e.g. at a ball and socket junction in a robot. I did cool things like assembling a Bracchio robot arm and checking out their CubeSat in the clean room ready to be deployed to space. I also learnt how to use CAD (Creo) to design the mechanical case for a mobile phone. On the last day, I was whisked away to a big space conference which i forget the name of, but i got to see what space companies do and the extortionate prices charged for sandwiches at the lunch bar (£10!).
  • Ultimately, I learnt engineering wasn't the right path for me. An engineer's main priority is to build something useful; they seek understanding up to the extent that it improves performance. I wanted to understand systems more than this, so I turned to physics. However, I still like working on problems that are useful like neural networks, but turn out to need more conceptual understanding.
